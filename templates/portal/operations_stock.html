How To order is worked out

To be shipped counts the pens in every parcel planned for this week that isn’t cancelled: draft, held, ready or already dispatched.

To order counts the pens in this week’s ready parcels only{% if week_order.parcels %} ({{ week_order.parcels }} parcel{{ week_order.parcels|pluralize }}, {% if week_order.locked == week_order.parcels %}week locked{% else %}{{ week_order.locked }} locked{% endif %}){% endif %}. Pens aren’t kept in stock, so nothing on hand or held back as safety stock is taken off: what’s ordered is what the ready parcels need.

On hand and Safety stock are to be confirmed: once pens on hand are counted, To order would take off what’s on hand and add the safety stock.

In draft parcels and In held parcels are the difference between the two: they join To order once marked ready. Average a week is the pens dispatched over the {{ average_weeks }} weeks before this one, divided by {{ average_weeks }}. Next week is the pens planned for the following week so far.
