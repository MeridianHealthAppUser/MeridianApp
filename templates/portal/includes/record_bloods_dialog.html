What happens next

The patient gets a task on My Tasks to mark the tests Done once they’ve had them, and you then get a task to review them.

Setting ‘Required by’ will pause the subscription and shipping if the tests haven’t been done and reviewed by you a week after that date, until you mark them Reviewed. The patient is emailed about this on the ‘Required by’ date.

Subscription and shipping will be unaffected if ‘Required by’ is left blank.
