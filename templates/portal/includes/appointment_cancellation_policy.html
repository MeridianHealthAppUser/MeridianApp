{# In the Update booking status dialog, in place of the old confirmation tick (Nov 2026): the 24-hour policy, read before the dialog's confirm button. #}

Please note that bookings missed or cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled time will be billed for in full and not subject to a refund. A new consultation fee will be billed for an appointment that is rescheduled within 24 hours of the scheduled time. Appointments changed at least 24 hours in advance will be refunded or rescheduled without an additional charge.
