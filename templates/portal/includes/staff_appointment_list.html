{% comment %}A staff list of appointments in the format of a patient's My Appointments (Nov 2026): Calendar's Appointments tab, with the patient's name, and a record's Appointments tab. portal/staff_appointments.py builds it. The count beside Filters, then Upcoming and Past as pill tabs, then each appointment's date, time and length, its type, clinician and status. Actions are small buttons, in the order and size of My Appointments' (patient_appointments.html): on Upcoming, a booked appointment has Open meeting room when it's the doctor's own (greyed out until the room opens, with when it opens behind the (i), then switched on without a reload by static/js/appointment_video.js, as for patients), Edit and Cancel (plain, not red) when this person may, each in a dialog (appointment_edit_dialog.html, appointment_cancel_dialog.html), then Details, the appointment's page, where the note is written. On Past, the booked clinician's own appointments still booked have Complete and No-show, icon buttons with a tooltip, each confirmed in a dialog (appointment_attendance_dialog.html); No-show not once the consultation note is signed. Without scripts, Edit, Cancel, Complete and No-show open the same dialogs on the appointment's page. book_url, book_dialog and book_label add a button after Filters.{% endcomment %}
