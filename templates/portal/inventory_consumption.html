How the averages work
Last 8 weeks
Total units from {{ report.average_8_start|date:'j M Y' }} to {{ report.average_end|date:'j M Y' }}, divided by 8. Weeks with no dispatches count as zero.
This year
Units from 1 January {{ report.as_of|date:'Y' }} through the last completed Sunday, divided by the elapsed days ÷ 7. This includes the part of the first week that falls in January.
All time
Units from each product’s first recorded dispatch week through the last completed Sunday, divided by all weeks in that period, including weeks with zero dispatches.

These are historical dispatch averages. Starting AWC used for purchasing is separate.
