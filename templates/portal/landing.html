Our process

How It Works

  1. 1

    Complete the online assessment

    Submit your basic details and answer a few questions so we can tell whether you’re likely to be a candidate for medical weight loss.

  2. 2

    Virtual appointment

    Book a secure session with one of our doctors. After payment you receive a link for your 30-minute initial consultation, which takes place over online video call.

  3. 3

    Initiate treatment

    If your doctor prescribes treatment, sign up to start receiving your medication couriered to your door. Your plan and its cost are set out in full before your first payment. Cancel at any time.

  4. 4

    Virtual follow-ups

    Book follow-up appointments at no additional charge. A review over online video call at least every six months is needed to continue treatment.

Worth knowing

A prescription is never guaranteed. The questionnaire predicts suitability accurately, but your doctor decides at the consultation whether medication is appropriate and in your best interests.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are GLP-1 agonists?

Originally developed to manage blood sugar, GLP-1 treatments have also been shown to produce average weight loss of 15–23% of starting body weight in clinical studies. They reduce appetite through their effects on stomach emptying and hunger regulation in the brain, and are usually taken once a week using a pen-like device.

How much weight will I lose?

Studies involving thousands of patients have shown average weight loss of 15–23% of total body weight after one year. Depending on your goals and starting point, it is generally possible to lose more or less than this.

Are these weight support methods safe?

Under expert supervision, GLP-1 treatments are considered generally safe. Some people experience nausea, constipation, diarrhoea or abdominal discomfort. More serious effects are rare, which is why close medical supervision matters.

How much does it cost? Do you accept medical aid?

The initial virtual consultation is R630. If treatment is prescribed, subscriptions start at R1 995 a month for the first two months and R3 295 thereafter. An invoice can be issued for you to submit for the initial consult; medical aids generally do not cover GLP-1 medication.

If I stop the medication, will I regain the weight?

It varies from person to person. Building good eating and exercise habits while on treatment gives you the best chance of keeping the weight off if you stop.

Am I guaranteed to qualify for medication after my consult?

No. The doctor must decide during your consultation whether weight loss medication is appropriate and in your best interests.

Where is the practice located?

Our doctors are based in Cape Town, but Meridian is a fully online practice serving patients anywhere within South Africa. Consultations take place virtually.
