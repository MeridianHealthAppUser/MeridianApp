# Meridian local application guide

The application has separate URLs for each practice-wide page and one tabbed workspace per selected patient. Overview pages contain summaries and links, not every workflow. The current deployment is **Meridian Health only**: there is no practice selector or practice-management page. Existing Meridian logins and roles are unchanged. See [single-practice operation](SINGLE_PRACTICE.md) for the deployment boundary and retained data model.

## Your profile and account menu

Click your avatar/name at the top right to open **My profile**, **Change password**, your role-appropriate history/preferences and **Sign out**. On mobile the compact avatar opens the same menu. The duplicate staff Account sidebar section and practice selector are absent.

`/accounts/profile/` edits your own shared account name and shows your sign-in email, active practice access and account dates. It cannot change permissions, login email, another user's account or recorded patient/clinical names. Password changes require the current password. Patient **Account** remains a separate care-practice page for contact details, consent and preferences.

## Doctor and care-team pages

Metrics and cohorts uses four compact summary cards, daily activity lines, an appointment-status ring, monthly cohort bars and a recorded weight-change breakdown. Date filters and CSV export remain available for Meridian Health. Expand **View daily counts** or **All measures & definitions** for exact values. “Current snapshot” measures describe now; other measures use the inclusive selected dates. No payment results, missing weights or clinical outcomes are inferred.

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/desktop/`, `/mobile/` | Separate desktop/mobile summary entry points |
| `/tasks/` | Table of patient/general tasks, assignment, due dates, workflow status and arbitrary tags |
| `/patients/` | Searchable Meridian Health patient directory |
| `/patients/<id>/` | One patient workspace; separate patient-specific tabs with a shared header |
| `/patients/<id>/?tab=history` | Role-appropriate history, timeline filters and protected exports; the old `/record/` clinical URL remains an alias |
| `/consultations/` | Author-owned drafts and explicit final signing; signed notes cannot be edited |
| `/blood-tests/` | Request tests, receive a private PDF and record the requesting doctor's review |
| `/authorisations/` | Explicit doctor treatment decisions, pause/revoke and reviewed renewals |
| `/compounding/` | Draft, review and manual-submission tracking; printable summaries are not prescriptions |
| `/schedule/` | Table/calendar, available times, working pattern, time off and affected bookings |
| `/schedule/book/` | Staff booking for an existing patient, with doctor and patient clash checks |
| `/messages/` | Patient conversations, replies and mutually accepted appointment-change suggestions |

From an appointment's **Details** page, the booked doctor can record completed/no-show attendance after its end time. Administrators can cancel a booking; patients can cancel their own future booking with a reason. For a new time, a patient uses **Request a change**, which opens a message to a practice administrator (their clinician if the practice has none) with the appointment in the subject; the administrator is emailed about it as with any message, and moves the booking with **Change time**. Completing attendance does not sign a clinical note. Rebooking retains the original appointment and requires a separate accepted proposal.

Native **video consultations** are available to the booked doctor (from the appointment's details) and patient, from fifteen minutes before the appointment until its end. Patients have no page for one appointment: on My Appointments, and in the next-consult banner there and on My Treatment, **Open meeting room** is greyed out until the room opens and turns blue automatically when it does. Beside it, **Request a change** and **Cancel** each open a dialog with the 24-hour cancellation policy; Request a change then goes on to a message to the practice, and says plainly when the appointment is less than 24 hours away. Patients add an appointment to their calendar from the file attached to its confirmation email. Camera access requires an explicit Join action. Mic/camera controls, screen sharing, reconnect handling and in-app invitations are included; connection history is not automatic attendance. Read the [video guide](VIDEO_CONSULTATIONS.md) for local testing and required Redis/TURN deployment configuration.

In Messages, **Route / manage** can assign a reply task to a doctor, close or reopen a conversation. Closing retains its history and is blocked while an appointment suggestion is pending. Administrative task notes are not sent as a patient reply.

Clinical record access differs by role. Doctors can see their own private notes/drafts through their protected workflows; the shared record excludes private notes and drafts. Super Admins can read shared/signed clinical records, not another doctor's private drafts or patient medical-profile answers. Practice Administrators retain operational access without clinical note bodies. Cross-practice combined records are disabled in the current deployment.

## Working within one patient

Choose a patient in the directory. Their name, details and tab bar remain visible while you work. Tabs use `/patients/<id>/?tab=...` and only render the selected section; they do not take you to an app-wide page with a filter applied.

- **Overview**: a compact summary, with no full forms or conversations. Looking at it does not mark messages read.
- **History**: the permitted event log. Doctors and practice Super Admins see the clinical record; Practice Administrators see only an operational history without clinical note bodies or audit metadata.
- **Appointments**, **Messages**, **Tasks** and **Deliveries**: records and actions for this patient only. The Messages tab opens with no conversation selected and shows each one's unread count. Selecting a conversation shows its own message history, and only opened incoming messages are marked read.
- **Consultations**, **Blood tests**, **Notes** and **Treatment**: shown only to the appropriate clinical roles. Unsigned consultations remain author-only; private notes remain author-only while the author has the Doctor role. Generated clinical tasks must still be completed through their clinical workflow.
- **Weight**: the recorded weight trend and exact check-ins. Chart points are spaced by their actual dates, with first/current/change summaries. The chart covers up to the latest 300 check-ins; the paginated table still provides older entries. There are explicit empty and single-entry states, and longer notes can be expanded.
- **Payments**: existing local records only. No charge can be made here, and a local “paid” status does not verify a real payment.

Lists are paginated within the selected patient. A tab change never changes the patient. Booking, reply, note and task errors retain the draft in the relevant section. Global navigation such as **Patients**, **Schedule** or **Messages** remains the intentional way to leave the patient's workspace for a practice-wide page.

### History loading and downloads

The clinical history initially shows 20 events. Scroll its panel or use **Load older entries** to append older entries; normal pagination remains available when JavaScript is unavailable. Activity/date filters are retained and history is limited to Meridian Health. Times are displayed in South Africa time (SAST).

**Download Excel** exports the same filtered history snapshot, including older entries not yet loaded on screen. The workbook includes its scope and filters, excludes private notes, unsigned consultations, attachment bytes and arbitrary audit metadata, and writes cells as text. An export above 10,000 entries asks you to narrow the filters; it is not silently cut short. **Export clinical record** remains a separate protected JSON clinical-record download.

History links expire and recheck practice/role access. If the practice or your access changes in another tab, reload the record before loading more events or downloading. Neither loading more history nor downloading it changes a clinical record.

## Local treatment and supply

External prescribing and Zoom aren't connected. Payments run through the sandbox locally. Peach Payments collects them by card once the practice's credentials are set (DebiCheck debit orders and Stitch are hidden for now). The Courier Guy is used once its API key is set. See "Billing, debit orders and the courier" in DEPLOYMENT.md.

1. A Super Admin publishes a product in `/catalogue/`. For a main medicine the product is the pen the dispensary orders and sends (Semaglutide 0.25 mg). Products with clinical or supply history retain their identity, strength and safety settings.
   - **Doses** are what doctors prescribe: the weekly mg, the pen it comes in, how many pens go out how often, and the price and how often it is billed (Tirzepatide 7.5 mg weekly, 1 × 10 mg pen every 5 weeks). Several doses can share a pen. Supply is entered as the practice dispenses it, never worked out from the mg in a pen. A dose without a price can't be prescribed. A Super Admin changes a dose's price with the date it applies from: today changes it now; a later date keeps the current price until then, shows the new one beside it in the catalogue, and can be cancelled before its day. Every prescription for the dose follows its price; billing periods already opened keep theirs. Once prescribed or in a starting plan, its pen, amount and supply are fixed; add a new dose for those changes. A step-up or only-if-necessary tier is shown when a doctor chooses it.
   - **Starting plans** are steps of one dose for some weeks, then a last dose until the prescription ends (Semaglutide 0.25 mg weekly for 4 weeks, 0.5 mg weekly for 4 weeks, then 1 mg weekly). The questionnaire result shows each medicine's first plan with its prices.
2. A doctor prescribes from the patient record: Initiate (a starting plan), Renew or Change, for 4 to 24 weeks. A care plan costs what the dose in effect costs. A doctor can also record an explicit authorisation for any product in `/authorisations/`. The application does not choose medication or calculate a dose.
3. Saving a prescription drafts its parcels on the Weekly shipping list (`/shipping/`): on the practice's shipping day (Practice settings, Tuesday by default), the first on or after the prescription starts, then one every dispatch interval with the dose's pens. Pens are ordered at the **order cut-off** (Practice settings > Shipping, a day and a time: Monday at 15:00 by default) for the next shipping day, so nothing new is planned for a shipping day whose cut-off has passed: a change made on Monday afternoon has its first parcel the Tuesday after next. A renewal carries on the same rhythm; one made after the next parcel's cut-off (or after the pens ran out) starts on the first shipping day it can make. The first payment starts the treatment: its parcel goes on the first shipping day whose cut-off is still to come (paid by Monday at 15:00, the next day; paid later, the Tuesday after, with the parcels and payments after it a week later too), and the course's weeks start with that parcel while the prescription is valid from the payment. A change cancels the old prescription's drafts and holds any parcel already marked ready for review. Each parcel goes out within its own supply period: the dispensary can send a dose's pens once in each period, counted from when the patient started that dose.
4. Ad-hoc parcels: **Create shipment** on the Weekly shipping list opens a dialog for the patient, product, pens and date. Prescription medication goes out through subscriptions. Patients buy once-off items that need no prescription (catalogue products without *Requires authorisation*, such as creatine sachets) from My Treatment and pay by card; a paid order goes onto this list by itself as a draft parcel, marked paid, on the first shipping day whose order cut-off is still to come (as prescription parcels). Any parcel that isn't locked can be moved to another day, earlier or later, with **Change this parcel** on its shipment page. For a prescription parcel, choose **Only this parcel** (the default) or **This parcel and everything after it**: the patient's later parcels, their payments (still a week before each parcel; one that would fall before today is taken today) and the prescription's end, with any later plan step, move by the same whole weeks. It must be moved by whole weeks, to a day after the parcel before it whose order cut-off is still to come, and no later parcel may be locked (hold it first) or sent. The doctor's clinical log notes the move and the patient sees a *Parcels moved* card in Prescription changes. Orders appear in Operations > Non-prescription requests (`/supply-requests/`); one not paid by card waits there until the practice accepts it with a dispatch date.
5. Each week, on `/shipping/` (Operations > Weekly shipping), work through three queues for the week shown. The page opens on the week of the next shipping day; the arrows move between weeks. Parcel rows show the delivery address, contact number and payment details, with customer and medication views of the same shipments.
   1. **Pending.** Confirm and lock selected parcels after checking the prescription, active care plan, permitted quantities, delivery address, contact number and required payment. Problems appear beside the patient. Stock is reserved on confirmation; shortages warn without preventing reservation.
   2. **Confirmed & Locked.** Arrange The Courier Guy for these reserved parcels. Booking needs the API key and collection address in Practice settings > Courier. A waybill moves the parcel to Tracking. Stock is managed separately in Inventory.
   3. **Tracking.** Follow courier status updates. Use Record collection after the courier actually collects the parcels to record dispatch and deduct stock; a shortage needs an explicit override. If booked externally, record the tracking reference when recording dispatch. Saved dispatch details retain the products, doses, quantities, batches, delivery address, contact number and waybill.
6. `/dispatch-history/` keeps a list of dispatched parcels, with 25 rows per page and numbered navigation. Filter by From month and To month, then use the Excel icon to download all matching records as an `.xlsx` file. The download includes saved batch notes for tracing a recall, plus the delivery address, contact number and waybill. Operations and Inventory table tools consistently place the Excel icon after the count and before filters/search; hover shows Download Excel.
7. Delivery addresses: patients add theirs when they start their subscription, and can change it under Profile or Billing and payments. The practice team can enter or change it from the patient's parcel row. A parcel uses the address it was locked with.
8. Who ships: Practice Admins and Super Admins. Doctors don't (Practice settings no longer offers it), and a parcel always waits for its payment.

Invalid authorisations hold unshipped parcels, never rewrite dispatched history. Shared allowance groups combine quantities but do not imply authorisation for other products or doses.

## Suppliers and purchase orders

1. **Suppliers** (`/suppliers/`, Super Admins): each supplier needs its registered name, address, orders email and payment terms, and a VAT number unless *VAT-registered* is unticked. A supplier of prescription products also needs its SAHPRA licence number and expiry. Registration number, accounts email and *Our account number* are optional. The list marks suppliers with missing details (*Details incomplete*) or an expired licence (*Licence expired*). Neither blocks ordering.
2. **Products supplied**: each linked product has the supplier's code and, optionally, their description, size and unit price excl. VAT. With no unit price, orders use the catalogue reference price.
3. **Purchase order PDF**: *Meridian-generated* attaches Meridian's PDF when you email an order. *Supplier-generated* is for suppliers who only accept their own order form: you attach their completed form yourself. You can still send without one.
4. **Bill to and Ship to**: Practice settings > Purchase orders holds the billing name and address, practice VAT number, pharmacy Y number, accounts email and the doctor named on orders, whose HPCSA number comes from their staff profile. Ship to is the courier collection address in Practice settings > Courier, plus the delivery instructions for suppliers.
5. **Creating an order** (Purchasing > To order > Order): Review order shows each unit price, tagged *Reference price* when it comes from the catalogue, then line totals, the subtotal, VAT (15%, or 0% for a supplier that isn't VAT-registered) and the total. Prices and the supplier, bill-to and ship-to details are saved on the order when you create it, so later edits never change an order already sent.
6. **The PDF** shows the order facts, the Supplier, Bill to and Ship to boxes, the lines with prices, the totals, payment terms, *Authorised by* and, for cold-chain products, a refrigeration note. Receipt progress stays in Meridian and in the Excel download.
7. **Emailing** (the envelope icon on an order): To starts with the supplier's orders email. Add CC or BCC, edit the message and add up to 5 files (10 MB in total). The preview shows the PDF, or the order form you uploaded. Replies go to whoever sent the email. With no email server configured (`DJANGO_EMAIL_HOST`), local emails are printed in the server console. Demo orders can't be emailed.

## Administration and insight

- `/leads/`: questionnaire enquiries, screening outcome, versioned consent and follow-up notes. Follow-ups can assign an administrator, set the next contact date, record booking interest or close an enquiry. They cannot create an account or change clinical screening.
- `/dropouts/`: paused/cancelled care plans and administrative follow-up history. Following up does not restart treatment.
- `/subscriptions/` (**Patient orders**, the second tab of Patient Records & Messages since 26 Nov 2026, with Subscriptions and Non-prescription requests; practice administrators find it on their Messages page): care-plan records. A patient's name opens their record (for those who open records); **View timeline** opens the subscription's week-by-week timeline as the patient sees it on My Treatment. Payments are on Shipping > Billing. Patients enrol, pause and resume, or cancel their own plan on My Treatment (`/patient/treatment/`). Starting a plan asks for the card it is charged to each period.
- A patient record's **Treatment** tab shows that patient's Patient orders: their subscriptions (with View timeline) and their non-prescription requests, without the Patient column. Doctors have the tab too, for the patients whose records they open (without Open request, which is an Operations page). From a subscription's **Open authorisation**, the prescribing doctor manages it; so does the Super Admin, who can renew, pause or revoke any authorisation, and renew any prescription from the record's Overview. A Super Admin who isn't a doctor renews it for the doctor who prescribed it, and the clinical log says who renewed it. Revoking sets the authorisation to Cancelled (as the patient directory shows it); a paused or revoked authorisation is restarted only by a renewal or a new prescription. The Overview then shows the prescription as revoked, the clinical log says who revoked it and when, and no more payments are planned. Its parcels are held; one the patient already paid for can still be sent if the practice chooses to (Confirm & lock on Weekly shipping). All patients' Record column says whether the patient's record is active, not their treatment. In the patient directory, each subscription's status is a coloured pill: Active green, Payment retry pink, Paused yellow, Cancelled red, Not enrolled grey.
- `/billing/` (Shipping > Billing): every subscription's payment state (paid, retrying, failed, card to add or confirm), its **Payments** (how many are settled and what's been paid, opening the payment history where discounts are given), each patient's payment history, and failed payments waiting for follow-up. Only a Super Admin sees the totals and **Run today's debits**. Each payment pays for the period that starts with its parcel, at the dose's price that day (a scheduled new price from its date), every 4 weeks by default (set per dose). The first is taken when the patient adds their card; each one after it a week before its parcel (on the shipping weekday), so a card declined on the day is tried again before the order cut-off and the parcel still makes its order. Paid after the cut-off, the parcel goes the week after and the course carries on from there: the parcels and payments after it move a week too. A dose change gives the period that pays for the new dose's first parcel the new dose's interval from that parcel, and the doctor's clinical log and the patient's Prescription changes on My Treatment say when payments now come. The last payment is the one whose parcel still goes out before the prescription ends: nothing is charged for weeks with no pens, and Next payment shows None. Renewing in time carries billing on; renewing after the end starts payments again that day. On a scheduled price's date, the daily debit run makes it the dose's price and asks patients on the dose to approve their debit order for it. A failed debit is retried on the days set in Practice settings > Billing (3 and 7 days by default) and on the order cut-off day for its parcel (the debit run is in the morning, before the cut-off time). After the last retry the period is marked failed, a task is created and the patient is emailed. A debit order covers up to the highest price left in the prescription. When a prescription is started or changed (not renewed), or its price changes, the patient is asked to approve a new debit order, and debits wait until they do. Practice settings > Billing can limit this to when the amount goes above what the patient approved. `python manage.py run_debits` runs the day's debits; schedule it daily. With Stitch, DebiCheck debits go out in collection files. Until Stitch gives SFTP access, a Super Admin downloads each day's file under **DebiCheck collection files** on this page and sends it to Stitch, then uploads the REPLY and OUTPUT files Stitch sends back, which mark each debit paid or failed. A debit with the provider stays pending, and isn't tried again, until its answer comes back.
- `/emails/` (Administration > Emails): every email sent to a patient or enquiry, including journey emails.
- `/settings/email-journeys/` (Super Admin): the six email journeys. These are timed emails for six groups: enquiries who completed screening but didn't book, people who had a consultation, patients in months 1 to 6 of treatment, patients from month 5 onwards, patients who cancelled, and patients whose payment failed. All of them start switched off with placeholder wording. Each email has a delay, a subject, a body and a button, with a preview. Enquiries and patients can unsubscribe from journey emails, except payment emails. `python manage.py send_journey_emails` sends what is due; schedule it daily.
- `/data-requests/`: no longer a tab (Dr Czech, 26 Nov 2026): patients raise requests by message. Earlier requests stay at this address for Super Admins. Responses are retained, and a deletion request does not erase records automatically.
- `/settings/users/`: Super Admin staff access management for Meridian Health. Linking an existing sign-in does not silently rename it or reset its password. The last active practice Super Admin cannot be removed.
- `/settings/practices/`: disabled in single-practice mode, together with creation and editing endpoints. A practice Super Admin is not Django's site-wide superuser.
- `/settings/screening/` (Super Admin, Settings > Screening questions): the questions on the public screening form. Version 1 is the existing form, set up for you. The overview shows the live version and every version with how many leads answered it. **Start a new draft** copies the live version; the draft editor has the questions on the left, the one you are editing in the middle and a phone-width preview on the right (the **Preview** button opens it on a narrow screen).
  - **Core** questions (eligibility and medical-profile questions) can only be reworded; the app uses their answers. **Custom** questions can be added, reordered, changed and retired, up to 25. Name, contact, height and weight and the consent wording are locked.
  - A custom answer can be **flagged for review** with a reason. The patient sees the reason, and the flag shows on the lead's page for staff.
  - Work happens on the draft only; **Discard** deletes it. **Publish** makes it the live version: new enquiries on the website and the WordPress page get the new questions, while leads that already answered keep the version they answered. The preview is the same form with nothing submitted. On a live server a flag also shows to staff the same way as locally; the draft preview never creates a lead.
- `/settings/policies/` (Policy versions): the **Terms and Conditions** and the **Privacy Policy** the screening questionnaire is submitted with, kept apart from the practice's website. Publish a new version by choosing the document, pasting the approved text and an effective date: it is numbered for you (1, 2, 3) and earlier versions never change. Status shows **Active** (the version in effect today), **Scheduled**, **Replaced** or **Inactive**; **Read version** opens the whole text. At the end of the questionnaire the visitor reads each in a dialog and ticks one box to accept both; each acceptance keeps a copy of the exact wording accepted, which the lead's page shows under *Read what was accepted*.
- `/review-rules/`: planning settings and an explicit local reminder check. It creates deduplicated doctor reminders and can hold invalid unshipped supply. A reminder is not a completed review and never extends an authorisation or orders tests.
- `/metrics/` (Insights): Dr Czech's measures month by month. Each chart over time has its own **Notes** button: a note (a date and a line of text, such as a price change) marks its month on that chart only. **Each doctor, per month** switches between *Consults and signups* (a colour per doctor, consults solid and signups dashed), *Consults per signup* and *Semaglutide to Tirzepatide* (a gap in a month with nothing to divide by). A month with historical data uses its new bookings and has no follow-ups. Cohorts' fixed-month trends share one Notes button. Unrecorded cost/payment inputs are not guessed.
- `/activity-statements/`: doctor-owned statements and Super Admin preparation/approval using explicitly entered rates (no tick box to prepare a draft). Completed appointments and saved messages are counted. Approved snapshots are immutable. No money is transferred or marked paid.
- `/account/access-history/`: your own scoped, sanitised access/activity history.
- Light or dark: the account menu's **Appearance** follows the device's setting by default, or can be set to Light or Dark. The choice is saved in that browser.

The local review command is available for a configured scheduler or manual use:

## Patient pages

The patient sidebar has five destinations: Home, My Treatment, My Appointments, My Messages (with the unread count) and My Medical Information. My profile is in the account menu. Medical information has its own tab; My Treatment shows the full-width subscription, previously prescribed medication and, below them, Once-off items: products that need no prescription, bought through a basket and paid once by card.

Home shows the authorisation and next consult, what is on its way, and the recorded weight chart. Its only form logs a weight check-in, and it never marks a conversation read. My Treatment lists tasks raised from the record, such as booking a follow-up before an authorisation lapses; each one disappears once the record changes. *Your treatment over time* opens the week-by-week timeline: week 0 holds the prescription and first payment, and week 1 starts with the first pens; each week shows the doctor's decisions, parcels, dose and payments. At the top of the timeline, **Prescription changes** opens a list of each change the doctor made, oldest first as on the timeline, each a short card with the week it was made in: one line for what changed (5 mg weekly → 7.5 mg weekly), then its first shipment, payment schedule, next payment and how long it was prescribed for, each day with its timeline week in a grey pill. What moved the course by itself (a late first payment, a missed payment paid late, the practice moving parcels) has a card of its own; a prescription the practice moved still shows the weeks the doctor prescribed, to its new end. Clicking a decision on the timeline (the history icon on a purple chip) opens the list with that change outlined. Dates show the day of the week ("Wed, 26 Aug") and doctors their title ("Dr Sam Marchant"; Dr when their profile has none, since only doctors prescribe). The doctor's reason stays on the clinical record. There is no Updates page in the patient menu.

- Book an available 15-minute follow-up; download a private `.ics` calendar entry or suggest a new time in Messages. The initial consultation is 30 minutes and is booked during onboarding.
- See My Medical Information read-only: height, weight and BMI (each with where the patient started, from Summary data), and the medication, allergies, history and operations their doctor records in Summary data. Patients tell their doctor about a change in Messages; they no longer fill in a medical profile themselves (removed 4 Nov 2026 at the practice's request), and answers saved before then stay readable on the clinician's record.
- Record weight and view the actual chart. For a recorded treatment course, a separate illustrative 500 g–1 kg weekly reference starts after the four-week starter period and is limited to the course and a short illustration window.
- Upload a PDF against an owned blood-test request. Reports are private downloads, not public media links; clinician review notes are not shown to the patient.
- Buy once-off items from My Treatment: Add to basket, then the basket's *Pay by card* places the order to the delivery address on file. Once paid, it goes out with the first parcels whose order cut-off is still to come (the basket says which day). *My orders* shows each order's payment and parcel; an order can be cancelled before it is accepted, and cancelling doesn't refund a payment automatically.
- Review subscription deliveries, and view or download issued/paid subscription and consultation invoices from Billing and payments.
- Request personal-detail changes through an in-app message to practice administrators (or Super Admins if no practice administrator is available), edit the delivery address, change the shared-login password, and manage care-email preferences from My profile. The list of data requests is no longer shown there. Existing marketing opt-ins can be withdrawn; new opt-ins are not offered, and historical consent records remain intact.

Submitting the anonymous `/screening/` creates a **lead only**. A cleared demo lead can open the checkout, choose an initial consultation time and create a login. Card and EFT payments are not connected and no card details are collected. Only the checkout test code (`CHECKOUT_TEST_CODE`, `devtest` locally) brings the fee to R0 and converts the lead into a booked, signed-in patient with a paid invoice. The practice needs a consultation fee in **Practice settings** and at least one doctor with open times.

## Before real patient use

This is a locally working application, not clinical/regulatory approval. Review screening criteria, consent text, prescribing/dispensing governance, record retention, security controls, backup/restore, file scanning and operational procedures before live deployment. PDF upload validation checks basic format and size; it is not malware scanning. Public policy pages show saved practice documents and do not supply legal advice or invented approved notices.

Repository and deployment configuration do not mean the application has been deployed. Payment processing, outbound email and automatic paid lead-to-account conversion remain unavailable by design.
